Why this exists

Between homework and tuition, kids ran out of room to be themselves. And parents ran out of ways to see it.

No ranks.
No answer key.
Just a thing to make.

Side Quests is the third space, not home and not school, where a kid picks a quest, gets stuck, gets help, and finishes something they're proud of. And where a parent finally gets to watch it happen.

How a quest works

Every quest has three moves.

01

Pick it

Kids choose from open quests or bring their own: a game, a film, a gadget, a brand, a story. Interest first, always.

02

Build it

Real tools, real mistakes, a mentor nearby but never hovering. Stuck is part of the game. You unlock the next step by doing.

03

Show it

Every quest ends with a reveal. Kids present what they made to people who matter. Proof beats a report card.

What we believe

Someone they're proud of.

Kids choose.

Passion can't be assigned. We start from what a kid already cares about, then widen from there.

Make it real.

Real things beat practice problems. We care about what a kid made, not a certificate for sitting through it.

Freedom, with a guide.

Time and space to explore, with mentors who ask better questions instead of handing over answers.

Tools, not shortcuts.

AI and tech are there to stretch a kid's idea, never to do the thinking for them.

Questions

Good things to ask.

Who is Side Quests for?
Kids aged 10–14. Quests flex to the kid, so first-timers and long-time tinkerers are equally welcome.
Where does it happen?
At STEAM Varsity in Sahakar Nagar, Bengaluru.
Is this tuition or a coaching class?
No. There's no syllabus, no exams and no ranks. Side Quests sits alongside school, not in place of it.
Do I need to stay?
The Saturday workshop is a parent and kid session, so yes, you join in. Details for seasons will be shared as soon as they're confirmed.
How much does it cost?
The workshop price is on its booking page. Season pricing will be announced soon.
My child is shy, or has never built anything.
Good. Quests start wherever the kid is, and nobody is graded.
Can our school work with you?
Yes. We offer schools a free enrichment session for students. See the schools page.
