The offer

Simple on purpose.

You provide

  • A room, 90 minutes after school
  • One teacher present throughout
  • A short note to parents, if you choose to share one

We bring

  • The quest and all the materials
  • Facilitators who run the session
  • A calm, kid-led room

Students leave with

  • Something they made
  • A quest card to share with their parents
  • A reason to tell the story at home

How it runs

Three steps. No paperwork mountain.

  1. Pick a date and a groupAny group of 20–30 students aged 10–14, on a day that suits your calendar.
  2. We run the sessionA hands-on quest ending in a short show-and-tell. No selling to students, ever.
  3. Share the invite, if you likeParents hear about a Saturday parent and kid workshop. Only what you choose to pass on.

Alongside school, never against it.

Side Quests doesn't replace anything you teach. It gives students a different room to be curious in, and gives your parents a reason to notice that you're investing in more than marks.

Safety & consent

Your students, your rules.

A teacher in the room

A member of your staff is present for the whole session.

Consent first

We only photograph or film students with consent from the school and parents.

No hard sell

Nothing is sold to students during the session. Parents are only contacted through you.

Questions

What schools ask us.

What age group is it for?
Students aged 10–14, roughly grades 5 to 9.
How many students can join?
20–30 for a single session.
Does it cost the school anything?
No. The enrichment session is free for the school.
Who is behind Side Quests?
Side Quests is run by Aditi in partnership with STEAM Varsity, Bengaluru.
