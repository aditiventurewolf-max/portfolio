**Subject:** The brand my vet hates, and my cat will actually eat

Hello,

I have a cat called Shiva and I have tried every single brand on the market for
him. Every one. Having a picky eater is not for the faint hearted. You show him
something, he decides.

The one he decides on is always the one my vet does not like. Too many things in
it. And his mood moves, so he loves chicken one day and refuses it the next,
which means waste every week.

I cannot take the judgy eyes off my vet any more when I tell him what I feed my
kid. But he will not touch any of the fancier, more expensive natural brands.

That is the actual problem in this category and you are closer to it than anyone.
Healthy food loses the taste test, because the animal cannot taste the
preservatives and whatever else goes into the food they already like. And the
better the quality gets, the worse the price gets, on a product people buy
forever. Wet food most of all.
