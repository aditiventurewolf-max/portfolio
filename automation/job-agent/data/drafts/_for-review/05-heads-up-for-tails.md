**Subject:** The bit that happens after they buy the bed

Rashi,

I foster cats. Last year I spent weeks going to the vet every other day because I
knew something was wrong with one of them, and being handled, politely, like an
anxious owner. It ended with me sitting in the clinic for an entire day while they
kept her in observation. Then they found it. Then they listened.

What stayed with me was not the vets. It was that I only knew to keep pushing
because I had read enough to know I should. Most people have not. Most people do
not know that lilies will kill a cat.

They are not careless. They are uninformed. Those are different problems and only
one of them is solved by a better product.

You have the shelf space and the trust in this country that nobody else has. The
informing layer sitting on top of that seems like the obvious thing that has not
been built yet.

I run the Founder's Office at Bounce. Eight workstreams, no team, no budget,
problem to live, including a company-wide rebrand and a retail sell-through line
built from zero. I foster with Bangalore Cat Squad.
