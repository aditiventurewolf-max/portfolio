**Subject:** Who makes a Nook work on a Tuesday

operating problem than a school with all three. Somebody still has to make a Nook
work on a Tuesday.

That is the part I would want. Not the philosophy, the running of it.

I am in the Founder's Office at Bounce, which means I own ten workstreams end

holding things together when nobody has to say yes to you.

Nooks in five countries all have to work without you in the room. I am curious
