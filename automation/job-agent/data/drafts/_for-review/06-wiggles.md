**Subject:** Most people care, they just do not know enough

Hello,

I foster cats. Last year one of them was sick and I spent weeks going to the vet
every other day, knowing something was wrong and being treated like an anxious
owner. Her whole history was a folder of paper I carried between clinics, so
every vet started from zero. It ended with me sitting there for a full day while
they kept her in observation, and only then did they find it.

The thing I keep coming back to is that I only knew to push because I had read
enough to. Most people have not. Most people do not know lilies kill cats, or
which signs are the ones that should worry them.

They are not careless. They are uninformed. Those are different problems.

You are one of the few people already building across care and products rather
than one or the other. The part I would want to work on is the layer that tells
an owner what to look for before anything goes wrong.

I run the Founder's Office at Bounce. ten workstreams, no team, no budget,
problem statement to live. I wrote the Daily Brief piece on why 90 percent of
Indian pet spending is still offline. I foster with Bangalore Cat Squad.

Worth 15 minutes?

Aditi
