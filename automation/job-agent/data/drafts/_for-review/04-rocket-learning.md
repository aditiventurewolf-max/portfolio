**Subject:** The parents care already

Hello,

I have spent the last year in a founder's office, which mostly means owning
things nobody else has to own. Eight workstreams, no team, no standing budget.
Some days that is unloading a truck of helmets. Some days it is going through
repair data to work out why a cost line is unpredictable and how to forecast it.

The reason I am writing to you rather than to another edtech is something I
worked out from a completely unrelated place.

I foster cats. I spent weeks taking one to the vet knowing something was wrong and
being treated like an anxious owner, until I sat in the clinic for an entire day
and they finally found it. What I took from that was not that the vets were bad.
It was that I only knew to push because I had read enough to. Most people have
not. They are not careless. They just do not know enough, and nobody has handed
them the thing that would tell them.

That is the assumption underneath your whole model as I understand it. The parents
already care. What is missing is the tooling around them.

I would like to work on that, on the operating side rather than the pedagogy. Is
there a version of this where I would be useful?
