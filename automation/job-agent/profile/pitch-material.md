# Pitch material, in her words

Raw material for cold approaches, given by her on 2026-08-26. Use the scenes and
the conclusions. Do not summarise them into abstractions, that is what kills them.

She has two cats: **Shiva** and **Ballu**. Use either, both are real.

---

## Pet food, and why healthy food loses

She has tried every single brand on the market for Shiva. Every one. He is a
picky eater. You show it to him and he chooses.

Whatever he chooses is always the one the vet does not like. Too many things in
it. And his mood fluctuates, so he loves chicken one day and refuses it the next,
which means **waste**.

Her line, and it is the best one she has given: *"I can't take the judgy eyes off
my vet anymore when I tell him what I feed my kid Shiva. But what can I do? He
does not eat any of the fancier, more expensive natural brands."*

### The problem she wants to work on

**How do you make healthy food tasty?** There is a real gap. Cats do not like
healthy food because they cannot taste the preservatives and whatever else gets
added to the stuff they do like.

And it has to be done **without making it expensive**. Better quality currently
means worse prices, and this is a product you buy forever, so the cost compounds.
Wet food especially.

**Built for the Indian market**, not another imported brand. She thinks the market
here is enormous and under-served by exactly this.

## Pet services: boarding, grooming, and the vet

### Boarding terrifies her

She cannot know whether a boarder will have the same commitment to Shiva's safety
and wellbeing that she does. She cannot know whether the other cats there are
carrying anything.

### Grooming made her give up and do it herself

Grooming is expensive **and** the care is worse than you would get anywhere else,
especially when the owner is not physically present. She now buys grooming kits
and does it herself, because nobody else showed that level of care and comfort.

Her framing: *"We have so many spa services for humans. Spa services for animals
have so much to cover."* Making the animal comfortable is not something she has
seen clinics prioritise.

### The vet is a different kind of problem, and this is the sharp insight

Grooming, boarding and sitting are **readjustable**. If a provider is bad you
switch, or you do it yourself.

The vet is not. When the treatment from an assistant is poor, you have to deal
with it and mitigate as best you can.

**It is a stakeholder agency problem.** Their interest is finishing this
appointment so they can take the next one. Yours is your animal. Those are not
the same interest, and the structure does not align them.

That is a business observation rather than a complaint, and it travels to any
company running clinics or a services marketplace.

## Education

Usable in outreach. Contains nothing about Beyond School, so it is safe.

Education is not being handled well right now, and private companies have to step
in, because government reform aims at lifting the bottom tier up to the middle.

**The gap is at the other end.** High-achieving students get pulled down to
medium.

And she does not mean the socially recognised high achievers. Not the IIT story.
She means **the people who never became high achievers because they never had a
chance**. They did not know they were supposed to take initiative. They did not
have the mindset. There was nobody to encourage them.

A normal classroom is built for the needs of the many, so it cannot reach them.

Personalisation exists at undergrad and graduate level. **Personalising K-12 is
the need of the hour**, and that is where she wants to work.
