## What this unlocks for us

  I can be aggressive rather than vague.
- **Ops process redesign with AI tooling.** She has done the real version. I can
  draft, she can tell me where it breaks in practice.
- **Anything requiring a rider's-eye view of Indian mobility.** She has the
  primary research; I have the synthesis.
- **Agentic systems.** She can specify and evaluate, not just consume.
- **Published-quality explanatory writing.** She has the bar, and a publication.

And these are things I should stop offering:

- Generic content calendars. Beneath her.
- "Here is how to use AI" material. She is past it.
- Deep engineering. Not her lane and not the goal.
