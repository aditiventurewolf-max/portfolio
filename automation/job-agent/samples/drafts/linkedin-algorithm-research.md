# How the LinkedIn Algorithm Actually Works

**Source:** original research across 318,842 posts plus LinkedIn's engineering blog
**Attach when:** content, growth, social, or anything where distribution is the
bottleneck and the brief wants evidence rather than opinion.

## What to say when attaching it

"I pulled 318,842 posts and read LinkedIn's own engineering blog to work out what
the ranking actually does. Comments are weighted about 13.5 times a like. More
than three hashtags costs you reach. Over half of a post's lifetime engagement
happens in the first hour, and the thing being measured is dwell time, not likes."

## What it proves

- Original quantitative research, not a summary of other people's posts.
- She goes to primary sources. The engineering blog, not the growth-hack thread.
- She can turn a ranking system into something a non-technical team can act on.

## The findings worth quoting

Two-layer ranking: fast first-pass screening, then ML models predicting per-person
relevance. Spam triggers: more than 3 hashtags, engagement bait, tagging at
random, reposting inside 10 minutes. The "ambassador penalty" when the same 15 to
20 people comment on everything you write. Weighting: comments 13.5x, shares 12x,
saves 11x a like.
