# Delhi's EV mandate is not wrong, it is incomplete

**Source:** her own draft, written from live operating exposure at Bounce
**Attach when:** mobility, gig, logistics, fintech-for-the-underbanked, or any
policy-adjacent role. This is the strongest single artifact she has for anyone
in Indian mobility.

## What to say when attaching it

"I wrote this after talking to a rider near our HSR hub. Delhi wants every
two-wheeler delivery fleet electric in four years. He would rather ride electric
than keep paying for petrol. He also has an EMI still running on the old bike and
no capital to spare. A mandate without financing behind it just moves the cost of
doing the right thing onto the person with the least room to absorb it."

## What it proves

- She argues from one real person and one real constraint, not from a position.
- She has primary access most people writing about gig work do not: "it's a
  spreadsheet on my desktop right now with someone's aging bike serial number
  in it."
- She ends honestly. "Genuinely asking, I don't have a clean answer yet." That
  reads as judgement, not hedging.

## Why it travels

Any company touching gig riders, EV financing, battery swap, or embedded lending
is living inside this exact gap. It shows she understands the unit the policy
