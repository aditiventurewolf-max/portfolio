# Template: negotiated-terms sketch

**For:** partnerships, vendor, BD, procurement, insurance
**Anchored to:** four-insurer evaluation for a 10,000-rider base at Bounce,
negotiated down to commercials, commission structure and hub operating flow.
Now live in-hub.

A filled copy is a DRAFT.

---

## Structure

**The three or four counterparties she would approach**, named.

**What each one actually wants out of it.** Not what they say they want. Volume,
distribution, data, a logo, a category foothold.

**The commercial shape that works for both.** Revenue share, commission,
fixed-plus-variable, exclusivity window. One concrete proposal, not options.

**The term she expects to be the sticking point**, and what she would trade for
it.

**How it operates on day one.** Who does what at the counter, in the hub, in the
app. Deals die here, not in the term sheet.

## Slots to fill

- counterparties
- each one's real motivation
- the commercial shape
- the sticking point and the trade
- the operating flow

## Length

300 to 400 words, or a four-row table plus the sticking-point paragraph.

## What makes it fail

Listing partners without saying what they want. Proposing terms that only work
for one side. Ignoring the operating flow.
