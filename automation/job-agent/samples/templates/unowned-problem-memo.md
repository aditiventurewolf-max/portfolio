# Template: the unowned-problem memo

**For:** founder's office, chief of staff, EIR, generalist, bizops
**Stage:** pre-seed to Series A, team under about 60
**Anchored to:** eight concurrent workstreams at Bounce, each from problem
statement to live with no dedicated team and no standing budget

A filled copy of this is a DRAFT. It never attaches without her reading it.

---

## Structure

**One line naming the problem.** The thing that is visibly nobody's job, seen
from outside. Not a feature request. A gap between two functions.

**Why it is costing them, with one number.** Estimated from public signals, and
say so. "From outside I can only see X, so I have assumed Y."

**The first thirty days, by week.**
- Week 1: what she would find out, and who from.
- Week 2: the smallest thing she would ship or change.
- Weeks 3-4: what she would have proven or killed.

**The one metric that says it worked.** Single number. Not a dashboard.

**The assumption that would break this.** Name it plainly. Being specifically
wrong reads as competence.

## Slots to fill

- company name, throughout
- the problem, from the diagnostic in `profile/work-samples.md`
- the cost number and where it came from
- the metric
- the breaking assumption

## Length

250 to 400 words. One page. A seed founder reads it on their phone.

## What makes it fail

Auditing the whole company. Listing three problems instead of going into one.
Offering to help rather than doing the thinking. Any sentence that would survive
a find-and-replace of the company name.
