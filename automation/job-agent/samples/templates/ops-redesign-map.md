# Template: bottleneck map and the AI-assisted rework

**For:** operations, programme management, process, supply, delivery
**Anchored to:** Scaler AI Labs, roughly 30% of manual processing time cut by
re-engineering delivery workflows with AI-assisted tooling, plus the QA systems,
training modules and assessment frameworks that held the standard.

A filled copy is a DRAFT.

---

## Structure

**Their process as far as it is visible from outside.** Five or six steps. Say
plainly which parts are guessed.

**Where it breaks.** One step. The one where work queues, or quality drops, or a
human retypes something.

**The rework.** What she would rebuild, with what, and what it plausibly returns.
Give a range, not a point estimate.

**What holds the standard afterwards.** The QA, the training, the assessment.
Automation without this just moves the failure downstream. This is the part that
separates her from someone who has only read about it.

## Slots to fill

- the visible process
- the bottleneck
- the tooling and the expected return range
- the quality mechanism

## Length

250 to 400 words. A simple step list beats a diagram in an email.

## What makes it fail

Promising a percentage without saying where it comes from. Skipping the quality
mechanism. Assuming internals she cannot see.
