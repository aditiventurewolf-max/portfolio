# Template: BTL activation plan, on-ground

**For:** BTL marketing, brand, community, activation, field marketing
**Anchored to:** Bounce. Hub-level accessories sell-through, the rider community,
the first rider rest stop, and a company-wide scooter rebrand carried from agency
selection through founder sign-off to print.

A filled copy is a DRAFT.

---

## Why this one matters

Most marketing candidates only know above-the-line and paid. She operates
below-the-line, on the ground, where the customer physically is. For consumer and
mobility companies in India that is the channel that actually converts.

## Structure

**Where their customer physically is.** The hub, the store, the campus, the
society gate, the delivery queue. Be specific.

**The activation.** What happens there, who runs it, what it costs per site.

**The trigger.** Why the customer stops. A free thing, a fix, a photo, a
five-minute service. Not a leaflet.

**How it scales past one site.** Who gets trained, what they are given, what the
script is. This is where BTL usually dies.

**What gets measured.** Footfall to conversation, conversation to conversion,
cost per acquisition against their paid number.

## Slots to fill

- the physical location
- the activation and per-site cost
- the trigger
- the training and the kit
- the comparison against their current paid CAC

## Length

300 to 450 words.

## What makes it fail

An activation that needs a big budget. No comparison against paid. Ignoring who
runs it on the day.
