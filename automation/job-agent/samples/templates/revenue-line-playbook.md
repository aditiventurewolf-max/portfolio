# Template: new revenue line, zero to live

**For:** GTM, growth, revenue, partnerships, bizops, monetisation
**Anchored to:** Bounce rider accessories, built from nothing through demand
validation, pricing, pilot, scale across every Bangalore hub, and training the
hub-manager network. Then again with fleet-as-media advertising.

A filled copy is a DRAFT.

---

## Structure

**The asset they already have and are not charging for.** Every company has one.
Distribution, attention, data, physical footprint, a captive audience.

**Who pays for it, and what they are buying.** Be specific about the buyer.

**The pilot.** What it costs, how long to a readable signal, and where it runs.

**Unit economics at pilot scale**, roughed out with stated assumptions.

**The number that kills it.** Below what does she stop.

**Who has to be trained or convinced internally.** This is the part everyone
skips and it is where these die. Hers scaled because the hub managers were
trained on sell-through.

## Slots to fill

- the underused asset
- the buyer
- pilot shape, cost and duration
- the kill number
- the internal group that has to carry it

## Length

300 to 450 words.

## What makes it fail

Proposing a revenue line that needs a product build. Ignoring who executes it on
the ground. No kill criterion.
