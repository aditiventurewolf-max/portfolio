# Template: market sizing memo with a defensible capture rate

**For:** strategy, market entry, expansion, bizops, founder's office
**Anchored to:** Bounce's next-city case. Rs 49 Cr per month addressable, 16%
capture derived from live operating data, replacing an estimate leadership could
not stand behind.

A filled copy is a DRAFT.

---

## Why this method is unusual

Most sizing work stops at TAM and picks a capture rate out of the air. Hers
derives the capture rate from something observable and then names what would
break it. That is the part worth showing.

## Structure

**TAM, from a signal anyone can check.** Show the arithmetic, not the conclusion.

**SAM, after the constraints that actually bind.** Geography, licensing, supply,
whatever genuinely limits them.

**The capture benchmark, derived.** This is the whole memo. Take a comparable
they already operate, or a public one, and show the ratio. State the operating
reality it comes from.

**What it means in their units.** Monthly revenue, riders, seats, orders.
Whatever they count.

**The assumption that would break it**, and roughly how much it would move the
answer.

## Slots to fill

- the market and geography
- the public signals used for TAM
- the binding constraints
- the comparable behind the capture rate
- their unit of measure

## Length

300 to 450 words, or a short table plus 200 words of reasoning.

## What makes it fail

A TAM with no arithmetic shown. A capture rate with no derivation. Precision
that the inputs do not support. Refusing to name the breaking assumption.
